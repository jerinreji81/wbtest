Usage notes
- Use worshipbase-logo-primary-light.svg on warm stone, white, and other light backgrounds.
- Use worshipbase-logo-primary-dark.svg on deep charcoal and other dark backgrounds.
- Use app-icons/app-icon-512.png for PWA and home screen icon bases.
- Use launch/launch-light.png and launch/launch-dark.png for splash/loading screens.
